# Incident Report 2026-07-02

## Executive Summary
This report details findings from an investigation into suspicious network activities. Multiple external IP addresses were identified engaging in brute-force attacks and active scanning. Two highly malicious IPs, associated with botnets and Tor exit node abuse, pose a significant risk of unauthorized access and reconnaissance.

## Recommended Remediation

1.  Immediately block IP addresses 185.220.101.47 and 45.155.205.233 at the perimeter firewall to prevent further malicious activity.
2.  Review authentication logs for any successful logins originating from 185.220.101.47 during the observed activity period (since 2026-03-11) and initiate account compromise procedures if necessary.
3.  Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all external-facing services to significantly mitigate brute-force and credential-stuffing attacks.
4.  Enhance intrusion detection/prevention system (IDS/IPS) rules to specifically detect and block SSH scanning attempts and common brute-force patterns.
5.  Conduct a vulnerability scan on external-facing services to identify and patch any open ports or misconfigurations that could be exploited by scanning campaigns.
