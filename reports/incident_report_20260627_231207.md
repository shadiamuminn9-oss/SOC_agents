# Incident Report 2026-06-27

## Executive Summary
This report details an incident involving multiple external threats, including brute-force attacks, SSH scanning, and communication with known Command and Control (C2) infrastructure. A critical finding includes a successful root login, indicating a potential compromise of administrative credentials. Immediate remediation is required to mitigate ongoing risks.

## Findings

| Indicator | Activity | Severity | MITRE Technique |

| `185.220.101.47` | Known brute-force / credential-stuffing botnet node, successful root login, vulnerability scanning. | Critical | T1110 - Brute Force, T1078 - Valid Accounts, T1595.002 - Active Scanning: Vulnerability Scanning |

## Recommended Remediation

1.  **Block Malicious IPs:** Implement immediate firewall rules to block inbound and outbound traffic to and from `185.220.101.47`, `45.155.205.233`, and `94.102.61.7` at the network perimeter.
2.  **Review and Reset Compromised Credentials:** Identify all accounts potentially compromised by the successful root login associated with `185.220.101.47`. Force password resets for these accounts and implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all administrative and critical system access.
3.  **Strengthen Brute Force Protection:** Configure and enforce account lockout policies (e.g., 5 failed attempts in 5 minutes) and rate-limiting on all public-facing services, particularly SSH and other authentication endpoints, to prevent future brute-force attacks.
4.  **Monitor for C2 Communications:** Enhance network monitoring to specifically detect and alert on any outbound connections to `94.102.61.7`. Investigate any established connections to determine the extent of potential compromise and data exfiltration.
5.  **Vulnerability Management:** Conduct an immediate vulnerability scan of all internet-facing assets to identify and remediate any open ports, services, or misconfigurations that could be exploited by active scanning campaigns. Prioritize patching and hardening based on scan results.
